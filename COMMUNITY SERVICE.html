Active Community Initiatives

Faith lives through service. Guided by our shared responsibility, these outreach programs are organized to support, empower, and uplift local communities.

Organizing local barangay basketball clinics and physical fitness programs to build character, discipline, and teamwork among youth.

Promoting environmental stewardship through community cleanup activities to keep our local surroundings clean and sustainable.

Food & Relief Distribution

Gathering resources and organizing outreach events to provide essential care packages and meals to families in need.

Digital Inclusion & Tutoring

Offering basic computer literacy and safe internet training sessions for students and community members.
