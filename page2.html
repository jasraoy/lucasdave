Faith, Integrity, & Digital Citizenship

Faith, integrity, and digital citizenship form a core ethical framework for navigating the modern online world. Together, they bridge personal moral values with public online behavior, guiding how we treat others, consume information, and represent ourselves across digital platforms.

Living as a "Light of the World"

To be a light in the digital space means choosing words that encourage, creating content that inspires, and promoting truth over conflict.

Ethical Responsibility

Recognizing that technology is a powerful tool requiring self-discipline, honesty, and respect for others across every digital platform.
