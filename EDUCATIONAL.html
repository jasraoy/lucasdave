Educational & Digital Ethics

"Teaching moral and ethical digital practices for responsible citizenship online."

Pillars of Ethical Digital Practices

As digital citizens guided by the principles of "Una ang Ginoo" (God First) and being a "Light of the World", our online actions should reflect integrity, empathy, and respect. Here are core guidelines for moral digital creation and interaction.

1. Online Kindness & Respect

Actively combat cyberbullying by choosing positive, encouraging words. Treat online peers with the same dignity and respect you show offline.

2. Honesty in Digital Creation

Practice academic and creative honesty. Always give proper credit to original creators, authors, and sources when sharing or building content.

3. Truth Over Misinformation

Verify information before sharing news or posts online. Uphold truthfulness to prevent the spread of deceptive or unverified content.

4. Privacy & Responsibility

Safeguard personal data, respect the digital boundaries of others, and use your online voice to uplift rather than divide.

Netizen's Daily Pledge

Before posting, commenting, or sharing content on the web, ask yourself these three guiding questions.

  • Is it true? Does this content pass accuracy and fact-checking standards?
  • Is it helpful? Does this post contribute positively to the digital community?
  • Is it kind? Does this reflect moral responsibility and respect for others?
